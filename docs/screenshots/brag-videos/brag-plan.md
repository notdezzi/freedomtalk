# Brag Plan: FreedomTalk

## What is this app?
FreedomTalk is an open-source, self-hosted Discord-style real-time communication platform (Fastify 5 + Socket.io + PostgreSQL/Redis/RabbitMQ + mediasoup SFU + Next.js 16) — servers, channels, roles, real-time chat with reactions and attachments, DMs, friends, search, WebRTC voice, MFA, webhooks, audit logs. 181 commits.

## The angle
"Own Your Conversations." — you built the whole Discord stack yourself and you can run it on your own hardware. The video sells **ownership and infrastructure**, not convenience: dark charcoal + copper "energy" mechanical aesthetic straight from the site, real product copy ("Deploy Your Instance", "Your infrastructure, your rules.", "Because your conversations deserve freedom."), and a recreated working chat UI as the centerpiece. This is not a generic chat-app ad — it's an infrastructure film.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black-charcoal screen. The site's tagline slams in as giant copper-gradient display type: **"Own Your Conversations."** A thin copper energy conduit line draws across under it. Hard, confident, readable in under a second.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The app, alive**: a recreated FreedomTalk app window — server rail on the left, channel list (#general, #introductions, #self-hosting), messages arriving one by one in real time, a reaction popping onto a message, a typing indicator, the "Message #general" input. This is the product doing its thing.
- **The stack, worn openly**: the real tech names as mono-type chips — Fastify 5 · Socket.io · PostgreSQL · Redis · RabbitMQ · mediasoup · Next.js 16.
- **Self-hosted punchline**: a terminal-flavored moment — `$ npm run docker:up` typed out in JetBrains Mono, then "Your infrastructure, your rules."

## Outro / punchline
FreedomTalk wordmark over a copper radial glow, the site's real sign-off line: **"Because your conversations deserve freedom."** Small footer: "181 commits · fully open source".

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result: **open a channel → type & send a message (simulated typing) → message lands with a reaction popping on and a friend's typing indicator appearing**. Recreated as a working app window in the centerpiece scene.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "quiet premium infrastructure film with copper mechanical energy"
- Interpretation: confident restraint — few scenes, longer holds, big type, no gimmicks. Motion is precise and mechanical (conduit lines, gear-turn accents), never bouncy. Humor comes only from the swagger of self-hosting a Discord-scale stack.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm confident bed with restrained mechanical accents
- Music: bundled "happy-beats-business-moves" track (mood: upbeat-but-tidy business bed); concrete track chosen at composition time
- Music treatment: start at 0s at moderate volume under the hook, dip slightly under the app scene's typing moments, final fade over the outro
- Music cue guidance: if the chosen track has a bundled preset under `<skill-dir>/assets/music/cues/`, read it — e.g. vol-1 preset: tempo ~120 BPM, strong cue near ~3.0s for the reveal. Target strong cues for: hook land (~0.5s), app-window arrival, punchline slam. Sequential reveals (messages, tech chips) snap to every other beat (~1.0s apart) — never single-beat text flashes.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; copper glow/presence may breathe with music energy — no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — soft whoosh on scene transitions, key ticks on simulated typing, a light pop for the reaction, one confident hit on the punchline
- Audio-coupled moments: typed `$ npm run docker:up` line (key ticks), message-by-message arrival in the app scene (soft tick each), tech chips appearing one by one
- Restraint rule: audio must never overpower the holds — the polished tone earns its confidence through quiet.
